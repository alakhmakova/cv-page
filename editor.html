How to edit

  • Click any text to edit it.
  • Tab or Enter: next field.
  • Shift+Tab: previous field.
  • Arrows: between fields and lines.
  • Enter in a list: new bullet.
  • Enter on an empty bullet: leave list.
  • Web link: change its address in the box below it.
  • Buttons right of the sheet: hide a section, duplicate or delete a block.
  • Button above a contact: hide it.
  • Faded section or contact: hidden, not printed.
  • Gray text is a hint, not printed.
